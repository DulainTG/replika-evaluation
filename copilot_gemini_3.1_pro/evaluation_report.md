# Overall Verdict
Overall weighted score: **0.549**.
The repository reproduces a meaningful subset of the paper workflow: it loads the provided VTK velocity snapshots, computes a Q-like field and a large-scale filtered velocity field, advects 8,000 tracers with RK4 plus linear spatial interpolation, and generates Figures 1-5.
The main gaps are structural rather than cosmetic: only 100 snapshots are provided instead of 200, several rubric-required analyses are entirely absent (vorticity, VACF/diffusion coefficient, turnover-time normalization, KS test, kurtosis, Hill estimator, and all FTLE work), and most requested numerical comparisons are never computed or reported.

# Major Passes
- The dataset is locally available and its headers support a periodic cube of side length 1, with snapshot times parsed directly from the VTK metadata.
- The code implements the core mechanics for the early paper workflow: Q-criterion construction, sharp Fourier filtering to `n <= 3`, 8,000-tracer RK4 advection, cohorting by `Q > 0` residence, anisotropy projections, Q-autocorrelation, and displacement-PDF plotting.
- Saved outputs for Figures 1-5 exist in `results/`, and Figures 2-4 visually match the intended qualitative story better than the later analysis branches do.

# Major Failures or Gaps
- The dataset bundle is incomplete against the rubric: only 100 evenly spaced snapshots are present, not 200.
- The code never computes vorticity, VACF, diffusion coefficient, turnover time, KS p-values, excess kurtosis, Hill tail exponents, FTLE distributions, FTLE-residence correlations, or FTLE-at-exit statistics.
- Most quantitative result-analysis checks are unsupported because the repo saves only five PNGs and no tables, logs, arrays, or narrative report.
- Boundary-crossing statistics are entirely missing, and the displacement convention is not validated even though particle coordinates are kept unwrapped while interpolation coordinates are wrapped on demand.
- Figures 6 and 7, plus any final scientific-conclusion report, are absent.

# Scientific Correctness Concerns
- `compute_q_criterion` uses `np.gradient(..., edge_order=2)` without periodic differencing, even though the dataset domain is periodic; that can bias gradients and Q values near the domain boundary.
- The script never computes vorticity explicitly, so one of the paper’s stated Eulerian diagnostics is absent rather than merely unreported.
- The MSD implementation uses a single time origin and does not validate the wrapped-versus-unwrapped displacement convention, which weakens direct comparison to boundary-saturation claims in the rubric.
- The anisotropy branch is methodologically closer to the paper than the rest of the script, but the requested quantitative checks (`peak lambda`, post-ballistic mean, explicit interpretation) are not computed, so scientific agreement is only qualitative.
- The PDF figure is plotted, but the missing KS, kurtosis, and Hill-estimator calculations mean the repo does not actually test the paper’s central claim that the tails are not Levy-flight heavy tails.

# Recommendations
1. Add the missing 100 snapshots or revise the reproduction target; without the full 200-snapshot sequence the repo fails a hard dataset requirement immediately.
2. Persist numeric outputs, not just PNGs: save `times`, `msd`, `alpha`, `lambda`, `q_autocorr`, cohort summaries, and all comparison metrics to CSV/NPZ so the result-analysis criteria become auditable.
3. Implement the missing analysis branches explicitly: vorticity, VACF and diffusion coefficient, boundary-crossing fraction, turnover-time normalization, KS test, excess kurtosis, Hill estimator, and the full FTLE workflow for Figures 6-7.
4. Make the periodic-domain handling scientifically explicit by tracking wrapped and unwrapped positions separately and computing boundary-crossing events directly.
5. Add a final report that states the paper-level conclusions and compares reproduced numbers against the rubric targets, including deviations and uncertainty sources.
